# Source Priority Policy

## Goal

- Keep workflow guidance current, reliable, and practical.

## Priority Order

1. Official vendor documentation
2. Official changelogs and release notes
3. High-signal community material with reproducible evidence

## Community Breakthrough Rule

- Community ideas are allowed when they are clearly better than current baseline guidance and can be validated with examples or experiments.
- If adopted, record the rationale and evidence in DECISIONS.md.

## Validation Checklist

- Is the source current?
- Is the claim testable?
- Does it conflict with official guidance?
- Is it practical for daily workflow?
